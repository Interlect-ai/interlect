# Code of Conduct

Interlect is meant to be a friendly, useful place for people who work with investment decisions and for people who build software. We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

**What we expect.** Be respectful and patient. Give feedback on the work, not on the person. Assume good intent, and say so plainly when you disagree. Welcome newcomers, including people who are not developers.

**What we do not accept.** Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing other people's private information, and spam or promotion that has nothing to do with the project.

**Scope.** This applies in the repository: issues, pull requests, Discussions and any other space that represents the project.

**Reporting.** If you see behavior that breaks these rules, report it to the maintainer through the Interlect-ai organization on GitHub or by contacting Phillip Pasqualato via his GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and fairly. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who do not follow this code.
